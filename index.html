How to play

  • Click a hero, then a highlighted tile to move.
  • Pick an ability, then a highlighted target.
  • Each hero moves once and acts once per round.
  • Water costs double movement; crates & walls block shots.
  • Banana peels stun enemies that step on them. Flank for bonus damage.
