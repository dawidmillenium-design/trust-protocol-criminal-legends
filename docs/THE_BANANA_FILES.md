# THE BANANA FILES — intercepted comms of MERHUJUS & BLACKHUJUS
## Codex collectible set · ASEPO "Meridian" intercept transcripts · DECLASSIFIED (mostly)

**Canon note:** all entries are voice-memo / message intercepts between **Merhujus
"Sygnatura"** and **Blackhujus "El Jardinero"**, transcribed by the task force
analyst who was supposed to be listening for *crime*. Humour-boundary rule applies
(BLOCKBUSTER_CUT §9.7): these never play adjacent to victim material. Each file is
an unlockable codex card in the build; Mr. PING appears only as an aggrieved third
party, which is his natural habitat.

## FILE 01 — THE CANCUN INCIDENT *(the one Internal Affairs framed)*

> **BLACKHUJUS** [image attachment: one (1) banana, very ripe, gone black, held in
> one enormous hand for scale]: *"look at my black banana. enormous. nobody grows
> them like the old foreman."*
>
> **MERHUJUS:** *"WHY would you send me this."*
>
> **BLACKHUJUS:** *"pride."*
>
> **MERHUJUS:** *"It never arrived. I route everything through a five-proxy VPN
> chain. Your banana photo has been through more countries than our cargo."*
>
> **[THREE DAYS LATER]**
>
> **MERHUJUS:** *"Sit down. Your banana went to an American tourist in Cancún.
> He replied 'nice fruit bro' and sent back a picture of a margarita."*
>
> **BLACKHUJUS:** *"good banana energy. I like him."*
>
> **MERHUJUS:** *"The ring traced the photo. The tourist is in protective custody
> now. His name is Gary. He sends me fruit pictures every Sunday. This is your
> legacy."*
>
> **BLACKHUJUS:** *"tell Gary the black ones are the sweetest. like us."*
>
> **MERIDIAN ANALYST NOTE:** Gary's Sundays are now a calendar invite titled
> *FRUIT PROTOCOL*. Twelve attendees. Nobody remembers adding them.

## CODEX CARD — COLLECTIBLE RULES (build notes)

- Each FILE unlocks as a **voice-memo item** drop; File 01 (Cancún) is the
  guaranteed first unlock and sets the collectible's tone.
- Gary (American tourist, protective custody, Cancún) becomes a recurring codex
  presence: one postcard per act, always fruit-adjacent, never explained further.
- Humour boundary (BLOCKBUSTER_CUT §9.7) enforced by the drop table: voice-memo
  items cannot drop in or within one mission of any victim-adjacent content.
- All intercepts are in-world fiction; the Meridian analyst's notes are the only
  narrator voice, and she has given up, professionally, on page one.
