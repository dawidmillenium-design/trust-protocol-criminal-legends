## 1. THE REVEAL (why this episode exists)

Campaign 2 ends with a question nobody asked:

> Act II's stolen cars were never resold. Act V's motorcycle parts never surfaced
> on any market. INTERPOL-grade paper trails just… stop at the crusher.

**Answer:** La Firma was never selling the vehicles. **It was burying them.**
Crushed car shells make load-bearing rings; motorcycle engines make air movers;
headlamps make a constellation you can walk by. The ring's third commodity was
never cargo and never memory — **it was the space itself.** The gap trade (Act VII)
was the retail arm. HOLLOW is the wholesale arm: **they sell the ground.**

**Pitch line:** *"Every stolen car was a brick."*

## 4. STRUCTURE — "NEXT EPISODE" GRAMMAR

Campaign 2 ends on M26's receipt printing. The stinger:

```
1:16  The receipt keeps printing. A second timestamp appears —
      dated yesterday, stamped from 40 metres *under* the port.
1:20  SMASH TO BLACK. One headlamp ignites in the dark. Then a hundred.
1:24  TITLE CARD: "NEXT EPISODE — HOLLOW"
```

- **E1–E3 (R-1, Vietnam):** PING descends alone; learns the network's first rule —
  *the ground has tenants.* Twist: the Spine connects to nothing yet; it is being
  **stockpiled**, like the cars were.
- **E4–E6 (R-2, Pakistan):** Merhujus buys a price list with testimony instead of
  money; the Bazaar episode ends with the map drawn — and instantly out of date.
- **E7–E9 (R-3, Russia):** Blackhujus's thaw siege; the corridor floods on schedule
  and the schedule is the confession. Twist: R-3's walls are **Act II's cars** —
  VIN plates still legible. Film I's evidence was holding the ceiling up.
- **E10–E12 (R-4, Berlin–Paris):** the compliance heist — beat Weld's building-code
  objection deck, serve papers *underground* (a summons must reach a place that
  officially does not exist; the game makes "service of process" a boss mechanic).
- **E13–E15 (R-5, NYC, finale):** the Loop is closed by *eviction*, not explosion —
  every tenant's lease is one testimony, and the Landlord falls when the client
  list reads itself into the record. Final image: the headlamp constellation
  going dark one bulb at a time, each bulb a closed lease.

**Endings (3):**

  from Campaign 2 gets its sequel frame: the throne, now underground, lit by

## 5. SYSTEMS TEASE (build notes, no engineering)

- **UNDERGRID:** a second map layer beneath the existing 24×13 grid — same engine,
  inverted palette (walls light, floor dark), LOS replaced by **hearing radius**.
  Zero new pathfinding code; BFS already works in the dark.

  decibels; The Listener converts leftovers into map tiles.

- **SERVICE OF PROCESS:** the R-4 boss mechanic — deliver a document to a location
  the UI refuses to draw. The grid square highlights only when a witness *names*
  it. Naming as targeting.
