## 0. Guardrails (implement verbatim — carried and extended)

1. All Campaign 2 guardrails apply (§5.3, §6B 1–11, §6C 12–17, §6D, §16 review
   gates), plus HOLLOW's tunnel guardrails 1–7 for the underground elements.
2. **No real religion.** The weekly visitor belongs to **The Saffron Ledger**, an
   *invented* mendicant order — no Buddhist monastic vocabulary, no real temples,
   no scripture, no real denominations. Same convention as ST-4's invented saints.
3. **The controlling substance is the campaign's fictional memory product only**
   (FLOR thread, §5.1). Zero pharmacology text; effects shown as UI status icons
   and dialogue aftermath, never depicted in use (§5.3-3).
4. **The Colonel is written as the ring's oldest victim, not a monster.** His
   compromise is chemical and documented, his complicity is manufactured. The
   thread's thesis, printed in the debrief: *the system punishes the compromised;
   the crew's job is to un-punish them.* No abuse backstory exists in this thread
   — none was written, none ships.
5. **Geography is stylised fiction.** "Issan border country" is a backdrop; the
   forest district, the installation and the gem seam are invented and match no
   real alignment (HOLLOW guardrail 2).
6. **The gem cache is a MacGuffin**, not a tutorial: no geology, no valuation
   methods, no extraction detail. Gems are how the ring *prices ground* — that is
   the whole of their function.

## 1. The brief

> *A border officer signs what he is shown and forgets what he signs. Find out who
> holds the pen — and give the man one clear morning.*

**Interpretation used (recorded so the writer never has to guess):** a fictional
Thai border officer — **Colonel Thanet "Pen" Boonmee** — has been the ring's
longest-running memory-product subject. Dosed weekly by a courier of the Saffron
Ledger order, he guards a forest installation he cannot remember guarding, built
over a gem cache he has never seen. The ring's joke — the courier's weekly smirk —
is that the gatekeeper doesn't know there is a gate. The crew's job is not to
fight him: it is to *wake* him.

## 3. New systems

- **DOSE CLOCK** — a friendly-unit countdown status; removal, not application, is
  the player's objective. First inversion of the campaign's status grammar.
- **WITNESS AWAKENING** — clarity meter feeding Dignity-Rules escort; composes
  with ST-7's ruleset without touching it.
- **ROTA evidence class** — schedule-derived evidence; joins TRACE / ARCHIVE /
  PORT TALK / HOSTEL / CIELO in the codex taxonomy.
- **Same-faces engine reuse** — the ST-17 recognition check fires in S19-2,
  proving the Deer and the Ledger share labour: one crew, many desks.
